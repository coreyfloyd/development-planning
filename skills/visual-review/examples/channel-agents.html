Why this exists

Before

  • One always-on operator polls a global inbox.
  • Channels lack separate visible agent identities.
  • Operations and projects funnel through one session.

After

  • A user message launches a bounded interactive channel agent.
  • Each role/channel pair owns a visible controller and expiring lease.
  • Headless jobs stay independent of channel conversations.

System map

Controller lifecycle

TriggerTransition or rule
Non-shutdown intent while dormantAcquire lock and replay into cold-start. A handoff may already exist.
Ready / launch errorReady becomes active; launch failure becomes failed with a notice and retry.
User event or work completionRenew the 60-minute lease, bounded by the human-idle cap. A self-dispatched job chain cannot keep a session alive forever.
Implementation in the new reply modeTransient worktree-rebind preserves controller identity and lease while switching workspace.
Shutdown, or expired lease with no active workDrain active work, absorb the handoff, expire and become dormant. Shutdown while dormant is a no-op.

Completion and block/reap gates use durable job records plus terminal-session state; agent completion events are hints rather than lifecycle authority.

Per-channel migration

Keep reply_owner three-valued: legacy → flipping → new. The legacy reader replies only in legacy; the new controller replies only in new. Stop the old side and confirm it has dropped ownership before enabling the new side. Rollback goes through flipping as well.

Before cutover, the new path stays read-only and proves ingestion, deduplication, recovery and draft behavior in a scratch thread against the source runbook's six evidence requirements. Durable intake bridges the no-reply interval; it prevents a lost-message window without allowing both readers to reply. Worktree rebinding and remediation become available only in new mode. Retire the old reader after the rollback window.

Review receipt and acceptance criteria

The historical source recorded seven adversarial rounds and no Fatal findings in the final four. Its severity trajectory was illustrative, not a fresh measurement; it stopped at an asymptote rather than a formal zero/zero pass.

  • Identity lookup timeout queues unresolved messages with bounded retry rather than dropping a genuine user message.
  • The ordered ownership flip prevents simultaneous responders; durable intake preserves gap messages.
  • The human-idle cap gates block/reap even when jobs dispatch more jobs.
  • Completion uses durable job and terminal state.

Acceptance criteria: a user message launches a visible per-channel controller with a bounded lease; headless job runtime remains independent. Registry validation, message recovery, lifecycle authority and ordered migration retain the safeguards above.

Residual source limits: agents must reconcile replayed work to avoid repeating completed tasks. Notification-only incident replies and dormant shutdown messages intentionally produce no conversational surface.
